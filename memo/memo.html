1. The Leak: Gross Theoretical Ceiling vs. Realistic Recoverable Loss

Across 5,000 leads (June–July 2026; 2,500/mo) at ₹60k revenue/customer and ₹900 CAC, the single largest revenue drain is the Pre-Demo No-Show Drop-Off (Scheduled → Joined). Out of 3,229 scheduled demos, 1,169 parents failed to attend (584.5 no-shows/mo; 36.20% no-show rate).

Root Cause & Empirical Target Grounding: Sizing requires distinguishing the theoretical ceiling (₹61.62L/mo if 100% of no-shows attended) from recoverable cash. In the dataset, leads scheduled within 24–48h already achieve a 77.52% show rate (22.5% no-show). Show rate collapses to 45.4% when lag exceeds 48h. Our 78.0% target is therefore an internal empirical benchmark, not an assumed guess. Closing this lag gap recovers 229.3 attendees/mo (≈ 172 wasted rep hours in empty Zoom rooms).

2. The Lever: AI WhatsApp Attendance Concierge ("Aarav") & Trade-Offs

The Chosen Lever: A 2-way conversational WhatsApp agent triggered instantly upon demo scheduling via CRM webhook (Wati/Aisensy + Make.com). Delivers:

  • Instant 1-Click Calendar Sync: Injects Google/Apple Calendar links (.ics) + 30s project teaser video.
  • Interactive T-24h / T-2h Confirmation: 1-tap buttons ("Confirm Spot" or "Reschedule").
  • T-10m Direct Launch: Pings parent with 1-tap Zoom link and laptop audio/browser checklist.
  • Recoverable Financial Impact: Slashes no-show rate from 36.2% to 22.0% (anchored to the 24–48h benchmark; 229.3 attendees recovered/mo). At historical 17.57% join-to-paid conversion, yields +40.2 paid conversions/mo = ₹24,12,000/mo incremental revenue (₹2.89 Cr/yr) against ₹35k/mo tool cost (68.9x ROI).

3. Biggest Adoption Risk & Operational Mitigation

The Core Risk: Rep Workflow Bypass & Parent Spam Fatigue. Counselors might mistrust bot statuses and continue double-calling parents, causing confusion, while parents might block automated WhatsApp alerts.

Mitigation Plan: (1) Zero Rep Disruption: Bot directly updates CRM custom field attendance_status (CONFIRMED, RESCHEDULE_PENDING). In morning standups, reps are instructed: "Green means locked; don't call." Reps save 172 hours/month. (2) Value-First Content: Messages are not generic reminders; they contain the child's customized coding project link. (3) Instant Opt-Out: Replying "STOP" triggers immediate unsubscribing and tags CRM DO_NOT_CONTACT.
